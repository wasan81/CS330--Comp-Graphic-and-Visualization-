Working on this project improved my 3D design and programming skills. I learned how to model objects, apply lighting, and control camera movement. I created realistic and visually appealing objects, like a house and a vase, while keeping the code simple and easy to manage. I also became better at solving problems, especially when fixing camera movement and rendering issues. Overall, this project helped me create interactive 3D scenes that are both well-made and attractive.
 
For my project, I followed a clear design process. I began by gathering and analyzing the requirements. Next, I sketched ideas for the 3D scene. To keep things organized, I created modular functions for each object, added lighting and materials for visual appeal, and developed easy-to-use camera controls. I tested the scene and made improvements based on feedback, concluding with a final review to ensure all objectives were met. My goal was to create an engaging environment featuring various objects, each with a unique purpose. The main feature is a house, which holds special meaning since my daughter drew it in school. I also included a vase with flowers, a candle for visual interest, and a green ball for a playful touch.
 
The Milestones approach makes it easy to manage, reuse, or update individual components without affecting the whole project, which increases efficiency for future work. I start with structured planning to outline the design and function of each part. This helps me focus and ensures I meet all requirements. I also conduct continuous testing and refinement during development, allowing me to spot issues early and maintain quality throughout the process. These strategies combine to create a flexible and efficient workflow that works for many projects.
 
As I progressed through each milestone in my coding journey, my approach became more organized and focused on quality. I made it a point to fully complete and thoroughly test each step before moving on, which helped me establish a solid foundation for the project. This method showed me the importance of planning and testing carefully. It allowed me to tackle each part with confidence and resulted in a polished final product.
 
Working with computational graphics and visualizations has taught me useful skills in coding, problem-solving, and presenting ideas visually. By learning to turn data into visuals.
